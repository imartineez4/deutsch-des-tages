Privacy Policy

Deutsch des Tages · Last updated 9 November 2026

In short

Deutsch des Tages doesn't collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app has no server of its own. What you do in the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • The daily words you've been shown, and the ones you mark as favourites.
  • The words you add yourself in My Words.
  • Your settings, such as your reminder time and wallpaper colours.

This is stored only on your device, and we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it. If your phone backs up its apps to iCloud, that backup may include this data; Apple keeps it for you, and we can't read it.

Photos

The app saves the day's wallpaper to a "Deutsch des Tages" album in Photos, and takes the previous one out of that album so only the current wallpaper is there. It can do this in the background. Older wallpapers stay in your Photos library until you clear them in Settings, and iOS asks you to confirm before anything is deleted. Keeping an album of its own is why the app asks for full access to Photos. It only works with its own wallpapers: it doesn't look at, copy or upload your other photos. Images it saved stay in Photos until you delete them.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to send notifications. The reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server sends them.

Pronunciation

Words are read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech.

Purchases

Deutsch des Tages Pro is sold through the App Store, and Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your card or bank details. The app asks the App Store for its prices and whether you have an active subscription, so it can unlock Pro. Apple may share limited details of a purchase with us, such as the date, the plan and the country, for accounting and refunds.

Statistics and crash reports

Apple shows app developers download and sales figures. If you've agreed in your phone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may also pass us crash reports. You can change that at any time in those settings.

Websites

Links in the app, such as those in Acknowledgements, open in your browser, under that website's own privacy policy.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app, with a new date at the top.
