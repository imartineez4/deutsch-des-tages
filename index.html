Help

Put today’s words on your lock screen

Open Today and tap Save to Album. To have your lock screen show each day’s picture by itself, set it up once in the iPhone Settings app:

  1. Open Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper.
  2. Choose Photo Shuffle.
  3. Tap the album icon and pick “Deutsch des Tages.”
  4. Set Shuffle Frequency to On Lock.
  5. Tap Use Album, then Add, and set it as your pair.

The same steps are in the app, under Settings → Photo Shuffle setup.

Clear old wallpapers from Photos

Each day the app takes the previous picture out of its album, but the picture stays in your Photos library. To delete the old ones, open the app’s Settings and tap Clear old wallpapers. iOS asks you to confirm, and they go to Recently Deleted.

Restore Pro on a new iPhone

Open My Words or Wallpaper and tap Restore purchases on the Pro card.

Cancel Pro

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Deutsch des Tages. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking: what you do in the app stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
